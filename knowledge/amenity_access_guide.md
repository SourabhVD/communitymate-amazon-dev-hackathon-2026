# Amenity Access Guide

Property: Maple Grove Apartments
Document ID: amenity-access-guide
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-10-22
Access level: community

## Finding Amenities and Operating Hours

Maple Grove is a fictional Richardson, Texas demo. All amenity times use America/Chicago. Read the current SQLite amenity catalog for IDs, locations, hours, capacity, access notes and administrative status. Catalog hours do not establish offered reservation slots or live occupancy. Capacity is the maximum demo group size, not a live count. 00:00-24:00 means all-day access, subject to credentials and administrative closure. Other listed daily hours apply unless access notes specify weekdays or other limits.

## Reservable Spaces and Sports Courts

The clubhouse, meeting room, study room, media room, pickleball court, grill pavilion and golf simulator accept exclusive reservations. Residents choose one through four consecutive hourly slots, subject to the configured maximum. Availability is informational until the booking transaction succeeds. The seeded demo week is October 23-29, 2026, from 17:00 to 21:00 daily for these spaces. Other dates may have no offered slots even when a facility is generally open. A closure blocks new bookings; it does not automatically cancel existing bookings or notify residents. Ask staff about a previously confirmed booking affected by closure.

## Fitness Center and Yoga Studio

The fitness center and yoga studio are shared walk-in facilities. No exclusive bookings, fitness classes, personal trainers, equipment rentals or live occupancy tracking are provided by this demo. Consult the catalog for locations and hours. Use a resident credential where required; the concierge cannot unlock doors or issue credentials.

## Swimming Pool and Basketball Court

The pool and basketball court use shared walk-in access. No pool or basketball court reservation is supported. Follow posted safety and guest rules. This demo does not monitor water conditions, weather, lifeguard coverage or crowding. An administrative open status is not a real-time safety assessment.

## Outdoor Areas and Pet Facilities

The dog park, playground and courtyard/firepit lounge are shared walk-in areas. Supervise children and pets, remove waste and follow quiet hours. Residents must not assume permission to operate the firepit; staff control its operation. Only the grill pavilion has an exclusive booking workflow. Pet access to other facilities follows posted rules and the pet policy.

## Business Center, Charging and Package Services

The business center offers shared workstations and printing during its listed hours; no free-print allowance is promised. EV charging is information-only: charging fees, activation, payment and live connector status require the external operator. The package desk is information-only as an amenity; use package tools for records after concierge receipt. This demo cannot track packages in transit, activate chargers, issue access codes or change equipment state.

## Guests, Cleanup and Accessibility

The resident host is responsible for guests, cleanup and compliance with posted capacities. Do not invent guest allowances, rental charges, deposits or waivers. No accessibility certification or equipment suitability is asserted by this demo; ask the office about route or accommodation needs. Amenities vary by location; resolve an ambiguous facility name before checking or booking.
