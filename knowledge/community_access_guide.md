## Office and Visitor Assistance

The fictional leasing office is in Building A's ground-floor lobby, open Monday-Friday 09:00-17:00 America/Chicago, closed weekends. No live telephone number, email inbox or on-call service is connected. Do not reuse the real contact details of research properties. Office hours differ from amenity hours. Public resources contain no resident directory, door codes or lockbox information.

## Lockouts and Access Credentials

For a lockout or failed access credential, use the property's verified management channel or visit the office when open. The simulator cannot unlock doors, verify identity, replace keys, generate codes or promise after-hours assistance. Do not request or reveal a resident's access code. In a deployed community, these workflows require authenticated identity and a selected access-control integration.

## Urgent Issues and Service Limitations

Do not rely on this simulator for urgent assistance or emergency dispatch. It does not monitor hazards, call responders or notify staff. For an immediate danger, use appropriate local emergency services directly. A saved maintenance ticket is only a received record, not confirmation that anyone has seen it, been assigned or scheduled a visit. Do not invent emergency numbers or property response-time guarantees.

## Accessibility and Accommodations

Ask management about step-free routes, accessible equipment or accommodation requests using its verified channel. This fictional catalog does not certify accessibility, establish eligibility or approve accommodations. Residents do not need to disclose medical history to ask for general accessibility information. No medical or accommodation details are stored in the resident profile.
