## Move-in and Move-out Coordination

For this fictional demo, coordinate key pickup, move-in inspections, moving access and move-out instructions with the leasing office during office hours. CommunityMate can explain procedures but cannot approve a move, change a lease, reserve a loading bay, hand over keys or submit a legal notice. No elevator or loading reservation workflow is connected. Lease dates and unit assignments come from the current resident profile; do not guess them.

## Utilities and Internet

Contact the leasing office for unit-specific utility responsibilities and available internet providers. Do not assume utilities are included in rent or that common-area Wi-Fi credentials apply inside an apartment. The concierge does not activate accounts, reveal passwords or change billing. Shared amenity access does not guarantee internet speed or connectivity.

## Waste and Recycling

In this fictional demo, use the labeled waste and recycling stations near the north parking area. Follow posted sorting instructions. Ask the office about bulky items, electronics, hazardous materials and any collection schedule; no valet pickup or special disposal service is promised. Report overflowing bins or damaged shared-area equipment as maintenance issues with a precise location.

## Parking, Pets and Visitors

Consult parking_policy.md and pet_policy.md for the demo's existing rules. Asking the concierge does not register a vehicle, authorize overnight guest parking, approve a pet or assign a parking space. No parking permits, vehicle records, pet registration or access passes are managed by this implementation.

## Resident Profile and Preferences

The current resident profile includes apartment number (unit_number), building, floor, lease dates, preferred name, language and notification preference when configured. Missing values mean unknown, not permission to invent details. Phone may be unset; example.invalid email addresses are fictional. Preferences are stored data only: this demo has no profile-editing workflow or notification delivery. The demo always uses RES001 and is not real authentication. Household members and other residents' contacts must not be exposed through public community resources.
