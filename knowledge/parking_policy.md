# Parking Policy

Property: Maple Grove Apartments
Document ID: parking-policy
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-10-22
Access level: community

## Resident Parking

These are fictional CommunityMate demo rules. Residents must park in their assigned space and display the community parking permit. Contact the management office about an assignment or permit; the concierge cannot issue permits or change assignments.

## Guest Parking

Under the fictional demo policy, visitors may use marked guest spaces, subject to availability. Guest parking does not authorize use of assigned resident spaces. Overnight guest parking requires prior approval from management. Asking the concierge about parking does not register a vehicle or grant approval.

## Parking Concerns

Keep fire lanes, accessible spaces without the required authorization, and building entrances clear. Report parking concerns to management with the location and time. The concierge cannot promise enforcement, towing, or a response time.
