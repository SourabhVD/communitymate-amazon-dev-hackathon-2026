## Pets in Shared Areas

These are fictional CommunityMate demo rules. Keep pets on a leash in shared areas unless a designated area explicitly permits off-leash activity. Keep pets under control and respect other residents using shared spaces.

## Pet Waste Cleanup

Under the fictional demo policy, handlers must promptly collect pet waste and place it in designated waste bins. Do not leave waste in walkways, landscaped areas, or shared facilities.

## Pet Registration Questions

Contact management for pet registration requirements and applicable lease terms. This demo document does not establish fees, breed restrictions, or rules for assistance animals. The concierge cannot approve a pet or change a lease.
